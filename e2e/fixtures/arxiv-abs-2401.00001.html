Title:Filtered Attention for Long-Context Retrieval

Ada Lovelace, Alan Turing
Abstract:Long-context language models often fail to retrieve facts buried in the middle of their input. We introduce filtered attention, a lightweight scoring layer that prunes irrelevant key blocks before the softmax. On the NeedleBench and LongQA benchmarks, filtered attention improves retrieval recall by 11.4 points at 128k tokens while reducing attention FLOPs by 38%. Code is available at https://github.com/ada/filtered-attention.
