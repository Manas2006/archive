# Marginalia privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Marginalia is a Chrome extension for saving, annotating and organizing research papers on arXiv and OpenReview. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create in Marginalia is stored only in your browser, in the extension's local storage. That includes your saved papers, highlights, notes, projects, topics, reading status, settings, the text of papers you open (used for AI features), figures you capture, and your Claude API key if you add one. The developer has no server and never receives this data.

## What is sent to other services, and why

Marginalia contacts these services only to do what you asked for:

- **arXiv (arxiv.org, export.arxiv.org)** and **OpenReview (openreview.net and its APIs):** the identifier of a paper you open or save, to fetch its title, authors, abstract, PDF, and (for OpenReview) public reviews and decisions.
- **Semantic Scholar (api.semanticscholar.org):** paper identifiers and titles, to look up citations, references, venues and paper embeddings used to sort your library.
- **DBLP (dblp.org):** paper titles and DBLP identifiers, to fetch the BibTeX of a paper's published version.
- **Anthropic (api.anthropic.com):** only if you turn on AI features and add your own Claude API key. When you use an AI feature (for example, explaining a passage), the relevant paper text, your selection or question, and your highlights or notes for that request are sent to Anthropic with your API key. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by its own policies and your agreement with Anthropic.

No data is sent to the developer. Marginalia does not use analytics, does not track browsing outside arXiv and OpenReview paper pages, and does not sell or share data with anyone else.

## Your control

You can export or delete all of your data at any time from Marginalia's Settings page. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. AI features are off by default and send nothing until you turn them on and add a key.
